Melo's challenge

Our challenge was to translate the engineering and disruption of a sustainable-solutions leader into a brand that's strong, modern and inspiring.

How do you communicate authority and innovation in a technical sector without losing clarity and purpose? Ekoenergy needed a positioning that could connect technology, nature and efficiency, strengthening its role in Brazil's energy and water transition.

Our approach

We dove into the brand's positioning and visual world to create a proprietary identity — alive and connected to the future.

We built a complete brand ecosystem, structuring the new brand around an integrated graphic language, expansive visual patterns and a verbal positioning that balances technical precision, authority and environmental awareness.
