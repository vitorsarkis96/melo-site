Visual Identity Packaging Design UI/UX Design Dermocosmetics 2024
Melo's challenge

Our main challenge was to reposition a specialized dermocosmetics brand — translating its essence of care, science and innovation more clearly, and presenting its technical expertise through a brand that's contemporary, approachable and ready to grow.

Beyond showcasing a technical portfolio, the challenge was turning Aromatici's scientific expertise into a brand capable of building understanding, trust and connection. We had to balance precision and sensitivity, making its communication clearer and more contemporary without losing the depth behind its products.

Our approach

We started with a strategic immersion to reorganize Aromatici's positioning and translate its scientific expertise into a clearer, more approachable identity.

We also took over the brand's ongoing presence across digital channels, developing planning, content and assets for social media — expanding its visibility and strengthening its connection with professionals and consumers.
