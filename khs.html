Melo's challenge

Our challenge was to position a specialized law firm as an indispensable boutique practice for entrepreneurs in the health sector.

How do you communicate technical authority and regulatory compliance without feeling rigid or distant? KHS needed a brand presence able to convey sophistication, clarity and warmth — connecting the rigor of Law to the sensibilities of the medical and hospital sector.

Our approach

We connected the rigor of Law to the sensibility of medicine to design a singular brand presence.

We created an identity system and digital ecosystem that reflect the firm's maturity. Every design decision — from the minimalist symbol to the website layout — was made to position KHS as a strategic partner for clinics and hospitals looking to grow safely.
